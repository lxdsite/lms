The Four Days —
One Sprint, End to End

15–19 July 2026 Hanoi, Vietnam
Success
sentence
"A venture concept we believe in, evidence-backed validation, a visualization of the product, and a 30-day plan we own."
Day 0
Wed 15 Jul
Arrival and check-in. No program. Pre-work already submitted (due Sun 12 July). Facilitators review submissions in the evening and agree coach–team pairings.
08:30–09:00 Opening Plenary
Welcome. Why Vietnam as catalyst. The collapse thesis named. Success sentence, tiers, and the AI rule announced. Groupings revealed; coach–team pairings named.
09:00–10:00 Idea Marketplace + SPARC selection Venture Sprint
All individual ideas go on the wall. Teams pitch, score every idea on SPARC, challenge the top two, and select ONE to build. Output: one chosen idea + one-sentence reason, witnessed by the coach.
10:00 → Sprint begins Venture Sprint
The chosen idea immediately enters its first AI sprint: sharpen the problem statement and generate a first concept draft. The build clock starts now.
10:30–12:30 NIC visit Immersive
Ecosystem briefing and tour. Each participant hunts answers to the field questions prepared in pre-work, and tests their team's chosen idea against what they see.
14:00–16:00 Company visit #1 Immersive
IT services company: how products get built, idea → MVP → iteration. Q&A with senior engineers — including how they really use AI.
16:30–18:30 Street Systems Walk Immersive
Old Quarter. One framing: what invisible system makes this work, and where is the friction someone could remove?
18:30–21:00 Convergence Council (dinner) Daily Gate
Each team locks its working hypothesis and declares its artifact tier publicly on the wall chart. Coaches confirm the choice is sharp enough to build on.
AI move (optional): feed the day's signals to AI for pattern extraction.
08:30–10:30 Framework Bootcamp + Persona Lab Venture Sprint
Two tools only: stakeholder map and leverage points, applied to the team's hypothesis.
AI move: generate 4–6 virtual personas (user, buyer, blocker, operator, investor) and interrogate the hypothesis against them.
10:30–12:30 Founders Panel + rotations Immersive
Three Hanoi founders: how they reframed their problem; what evidence flipped their thinking. Small-group rotations follow.
12:30–13:30 Founders lunch Social
Theme: "If you started today, what would you build — and what would you build differently?"
14:00–16:30 Company visit #2 Immersive
Product and commercialisation lens: systems thinking on real problems, rapid iteration, innovation rituals. Teams watch through their hypothesis.
16:30–18:00 Scooter food tour Social
"Insights are everywhere." Bonding and sensory immersion.
18:30–21:00 Convergence Council (dinner) Daily Gate
Evidence memo per team: why this venture, what the evidence says so far.
08:30–10:00 Pre-pitch Panel Venture Sprint
Each team pitches its concept to the other teams: 10 minutes + 10 minutes discussion. Peer pressure sets the bar for the day.
10:00–12:30 Build Sprint I Venture Sprint
Problem statement, user flow, value proposition. A working artifact — ugly is fine — must exist by lunch.
AI move: generate the visualization / prototype draft.
12:30–14:00 Stretch-or-Hold (lunch) Venture Sprint
Each team meets its paired coach. Forced question: stretch to the next tier, or hold? Decision recorded publicly on the wall chart.
14:00–15:30 Build Sprint II + Business Model Lock Venture Sprint
Fix the biggest gap from the stretch-or-hold call. Business model locks at 15:30.
AI move: draft unit economics and GTM options, then interrogate every number — real, generated, or invented?
15:30–17:00 Venture Builders & Investors Panel Immersive
Feedback on the artifact teams are actively building — feedback density, not more inputs. No new speakers beyond this.
17:00–21:00 Tonkin show + dinner Daily Gate
AI prototype + locked business model. Deliberate decompression.
08:30–11:00 Build Sprint III + pitch rehearsal Venture Sprint
Finalise the artifact and the story. Rehearse answers.
AI move: red-team the pitch — "you are a skeptical Vietnamese investor; attack this."
11:00–12:00 Lunch + refinement Social
Final pitch polish. Checkout completed before this block.
13:30–14:30 Commercializing Innovation (university) Immersive
How university and research labs commercialise; pitches from local startups. Calibration before the main event.
14:30–16:00 Final Pitches Venture Sprint
Per team: 7-min pitch + 3-min visualization walkthrough + 8 min direct questions. Panel: Alvin + Vietnamese investor + founder + executive coach. Direct, no sugar-coating.
16:00–16:30 Leadership Debrief + Closing Daily Gate
VC-style pitch delivered. 30-day plans owned by teams. 60-day report-out date confirmed. Certificates and photo.
