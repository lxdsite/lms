Sasin Connect+ EMBA  ·  Final Residential

The Four Days —
One Sprint, End to End

15–19 July 2026 Hanoi, Vietnam
Success
sentence
"My team leaves with a venture concept we believe in, evidence-backed validation, a visualization of what the product becomes, and a 30-day plan we own."
Day 0
Wed 15 Jul
Arrival and check-in. No program. Pre-work already submitted (due Sun 12 July). Facilitators review submissions in the evening and agree coach–team pairings.
08:30–09:00 Opening Plenary
Welcome speech. "Why Vietnam as a catalyst?" The collapse thesis is named. Success sentence, tiers, and the AI rule announced. Coach–team pairings named.
09:00–10:00 Idea Marketplace + SPARC selection Venture Sprint
Each participant posts their pre-work hypothesis. Cohort walks the wall and marks the three they would back. Coaches observe to inform Convergence Council facilitation. Ideas are scored against SPARC dimensions, grouped by theme, and teams are paired to themes.
10:00–12:00 NIC Visit Immersive
Travel to NIC. Ecosystem presentation by NIC speaker and tour. Insights: Vietnam as an emerging powerhouse — youth, talent, speed, agility — and how the government is driving a digital economy and society.
12:30–14:00 Lunch Social
Networking format.
14:00–16:00 IT Company Visit: OmiGroup Immersive
Industry Exposure Visit #1. Explore how they solve hard client problems, their design and systems thinking workflow, and how they go from idea → MVP → iteration. Q&A with senior engineers and product leads.
16:30–18:30 Hanoi Street Systems Walk Immersive
Hanoi Old Quarter. Observe informal systems: traffic, logistics, street vendors, payments, friction points. "What invisible system makes this work, and where is the friction someone could remove?"
18:30–21:00 Dinner
Convergence Council format. The wall chart goes up. Each team declares its tier target publicly. Every team leaves with ONE working hypothesis — written, private. "Where did I lead today? Where did I avoid?"
08:30–10:30 Dual Framework Bootcamp + Persona Lab Venture Sprint
Design thinking: empathy, reframing, prototyping mindset — moving from assumptions to insights to angles. Systems thinking: identify leverage points, map stakeholders, flows, feedback loops, and understand unintended consequences.
AI move: generate 4–6 virtual personas (user, buyer, blocker, operator, investor) and interrogate the hypothesis against them.
10:30–12:30 Startup Founder Panel Immersive
3 Hanoi founders. Focused only on: how they reframed their problem; what evidence flipped their thinking. Followed by small-group rotations.
12:30–13:30 Networking lunch Social
Theme 1: "If you started today, what would you build and why?" Theme 2: "If you started today, what would you build differently?"
14:00–16:30 IT Company Visit: Savvycom Immersive
Product and commercialisation lens: systems thinking on real problems, rapid iteration, innovation rituals. Teams observe through their hypothesis.
16:30–18:00 Scooter food tour (Optional) Social
"Insights are everywhere." Bonding and sensory immersion.
18:30–21:00 Dinner
Convergence Council format. Evidence memo for the team: why this venture, and what the evidence says so far. Evidence memo on Post-its.
08:30–10:00 Pre-pitch Panel Venture Sprint
Each team pitches its concept to the other teams: 10-minute presentation + 10-minute discussion.
10:00–12:30 Build Sprint I: Concept Sharpening & Visualization Venture Sprint
Problem statement, user flow, value proposition.
AI move: synthesize insights, market research, customer journey design, and generate the visualization / prototype draft.
12:30–14:00 Lunch + Stretch-or-Hold Venture Sprint
Each team meets their paired coach. Forced question: "Stretch to next tier, or hold?" Decision recorded on the wall chart.
14:00–15:30 Build Sprint II + Business Model Lock Venture Sprint
Fix the biggest gap exposed in the stretch-or-hold call. Lock the business model canvas: revenue, unit economics, GTM, milestone path.
AI move: draft unit economics and GTM options, then interrogate every number — real, generated, or invented?
15:30–17:00 Venture Builders & Investors Panel Immersive
45-minute talk. At this point, teams have built: revenue model, unit economics, go-to-market strategy, milestone path, and required AI workflows.
17:00–21:00 Experiential Dinner
The Quintessence of Tonkin Show. AI prototype + locked business model.
Before 08:00 Breakfast & Hotel Checkout Social
Participants check out before departure. Bus departs at 08:00 to Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).
08:30–12:00 Commercialising Innovation & Final Pitches Immersive
Session opens with a presentation on how university and research laboratories commercialise innovation, followed by pitches from HUST-affiliated startups.
Final Venture Pitches Venture Sprint
Each team delivers a structured pitch comprising: a 7-minute venture presentation, a 3-minute visualization walkthrough, and an 8-minute panel Q&A. The panel consists of the programme instructor, a Vietnamese investor, a founder, and an executive coach.
Leadership Debrief & Closing Plenary
Panel feedback on pitches. Cohort-level group reflection. The 60-day report-out date is confirmed. Programme closes with a group photograph.
12:00–13:30 Lunch Social
13:30 → Departure Social
Transfer to Noi Bai International Airport.
